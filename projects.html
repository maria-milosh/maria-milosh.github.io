Open Facilitation Library

As digital deliberation becomes more prevalent, this project works to study what good online facilitation looks like, helping train people and AI to support collective decision-making. We are studying facilitation techniques in synchronous and asynchronous deliberative groups, focusing on discussion productivity and representativeness of the full range of perspectives.

Democracy narratives research and campaign

A new initiative to compile, develop, and promote compelling narratives that increase public support for and engagement in democracy.

Teaching Civic Tech and Algorithms for Social Good

I designed and teach a course at Bard College on the normative, technical, and evaluative foundations of public-interest technology.
